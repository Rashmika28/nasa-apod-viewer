Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026 | Last Updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Prathibha Solutions official mobile application and website (https://www.prathibhasolutions.com/).

Summary: The Prathibha Solutions application is a simple, informational WebView app designed solely to display our business website. We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data or sensitive user information.

1. No Personal Data Collection

We do not collect any personal identification information from users of our application. Specifically:

  • No user accounts, registrations, or login profiles are required or created.
  • No names, email addresses, phone numbers, or physical addresses are collected through the app.
  • No payment transactions, credit card information, or financial details are handled.
  • No biometric, health, or personal demographic data is collected.

2. No Device or Hardware Access

The application does not request, access, or track any sensitive device capabilities, sensors, or storage:

  • No Location Access: We do not track or request GPS, coarse, or fine location data.
  • No Camera or Microphone Access: The app does not record audio or capture images.
  • No Contacts or Calendar Access: The app has no access to your address book or personal events.
  • No File or Storage Tracking: The app does not scan or access your device's photos, documents, or personal files.

3. Permissions Used by the Application

The application requests only the minimum technical permissions necessary to display web pages:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required solely to load and display our public website content from https://www.prathibhasolutions.com/.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required solely to determine if your device is connected to the internet so that an offline message can be shown if connection is lost.

4. No Advertising or User Tracking

Our mobile application contains no third-party advertisements, no ad tracking SDKs (such as Google AdMob or Facebook Ads), and no analytics trackers that monitor individual user behavior.

5. External Links

The app displays our public business website, which may contain links to our official contact channels (such as tel: phone links, mailto: email links, or WhatsApp links). If you choose to tap these links, they will open your device's default dialer, email client, or messaging application. Any information you voluntarily choose to send through external channels is handled directly by those third-party communication providers.

6. Children's Privacy

Our application does not address or target anyone under the age of 13, and because we collect zero personal data, we do not knowingly collect or maintain any personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time if our application features change. Any updates will be posted directly to this URL with an updated "Effective Date."
