Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026 | Last Updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Prathibha Solutions official mobile application and website (https://www.prathibhasolutions.com/).

Summary: The Prathibha Solutions mobile application is a simple WebView wrapper designed solely to display our business website. We do not collect, store, share, track, or process any user data or personal information.

1. No Collection of User Data

Our application and website do not collect personal or device data from users. Specifically:

  • We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or physical addresses.
  • We do not collect device IDs, advertising identifiers, IP logs, or crash logs.
  • We do not collect location data (neither precise GPS nor coarse network location).
  • We do not collect usage analytics or track user behavior.
  • There is no user registration, login, account creation, or user profile system.
  • There are no contact forms, comment fields, or message submission forms in the app.
  • There are no payment systems or financial transactions processed in the app.

2. No Data Retention or Deletion System

Because our application does not collect, receive, or store any personal or user data, we do not retain any user information. Consequently, a user data deletion mechanism is neither necessary nor provided.

3. Technical Permissions Used

The application only requests the standard Android technical permissions required to display web pages:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required solely to load and display our public web pages from https://www.prathibhasolutions.com/.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required solely to check whether your device has an active internet connection so that a standard offline screen can be displayed if connectivity is unavailable.

The application does not request or access camera, microphone, contacts, location, storage, photos, or any other sensitive device capabilities.

4. No Third-Party Tracking or Advertising

The application does not include any third-party advertising SDKs, ad networks, or analytics tracking libraries. We do not track users across other apps or websites.

5. Children's Privacy

Our application does not collect any personal information from any user, including children under the age of 13.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If our application practices change in the future, any revisions will be published directly to this page with an updated "Effective Date."
