Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026 | Last Updated: September 2026

Welcome to Prathibha Solutions ("we," "our," or "us"). We are dedicated to respecting and protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding the collection, use, disclosure, and protection of information obtained through our official mobile application (Prathibha Solutions App) and our website (https://www.prathibhasolutions.com/).

Google Play Compliance Summary: Our mobile app provides access to Prathibha Solutions' services, updates, and customer communications. We do not sell your personal information. We do not access sensitive permissions such as location tracking, microphone, or camera without explicit user consent.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information directly from you or automatically when you use our application or website:

  • Personal Information You Provide: When you contact us, request a service consultation, or fill out an inquiry form, you may provide details such as your name, email address, phone number, company name, and details about your inquiry.
  • Device and Technical Information: To ensure proper app performance and security, we may automatically receive technical data including your device model, operating system version, screen resolution, preferred language, and app crash logs.
  • Log & Usage Data: Standard access logs including IP address, pages viewed, time spent on the app or website, and interaction data.

2. Device Permissions Used by the App

The Prathibha Solutions mobile application requires only standard, minimal Android permissions necessary for regular operation:

  • INTERNET: Needed to load live service content, verify connection, and communicate with our secure servers.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Needed to detect whether your device is connected to Wi-Fi or mobile data to display friendly offline messages when disconnected.

Our application does not request or access sensitive device capabilities such as precise background location, contact lists, audio recordings, or photo galleries.

3. How We Use Your Information

We utilize the collected information strictly for legitimate business operations:

  • To provide, operate, and maintain our services and digital platforms.
  • To respond promptly to your service inquiries, quotes, or customer support requests.
  • To optimize the performance, responsiveness, and user experience of our app and website.
  • To monitor and prevent technical malfunctions, fraud, and security vulnerabilities.
  • To communicate important service announcements or policy updates.

4. Third-Party Services & Technologies

We may integrate trusted third-party providers to enhance app performance and security. These third-party services operate under their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services: Used for app distribution, core runtime services, and security diagnostics. (Google Privacy Policy).
  • Web Content Delivery Networks (CDNs) & Fonts: Bootstrap CDN and Google Fonts for layout rendering and typography.

5. Data Security

We implement robust industry-standard technical and organizational security measures—including HTTPS encryption (SSL/TLS) for all data in transit—to safeguard your information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. However, please note that no transmission over the Internet is 100% immune to vulnerabilities.

6. Data Retention and User Rights (Data Deletion)

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

Your Rights: You have the right to request access to, correction of, or permanent deletion of your personal data held by us. To request deletion of any contact data or inquiries submitted to Prathibha Solutions, please email us directly with the subject line "Data Deletion Request" at the email address listed below.

7. Children's Privacy

Our services and application are not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from minors. If you believe that a minor has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so we can promptly delete such data.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our legal obligations or application features. Any modifications will be posted directly to this page with an updated "Effective Date." We encourage you to review this page periodically.
